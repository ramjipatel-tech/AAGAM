2. Purpose
This SRS defines the complete functional, non-functional, security, workflow, data, document, payment and automation requirements for AAGAM Digital Technology. The platform is designed as a professional corporate website plus a centralized system for internship, training, placement, recruitment, project management, student services and official document verification.
The system shall support a complete student lifecycle: discovery → application → email verification → registration number → administrative review → selection → Offer Letter → offer acceptance → payment where applicable → payment verification → portal provisioning → orientation → internship/training/project work → completion → LOR/Certificate → QR verification.
3. Business Goals
Provide AAGAM Digital Technology with a professional and responsive public website.
Digitize internship, training, placement and recruitment operations.
Reduce manual application and document-generation work.
Provide a secure student self-service portal.
Automate official document generation and email delivery.
Provide trustworthy QR/ID-based document verification.
Integrate Razorpay for applicable payments and maintain complete payment records.
Provide staff and administrators with centralized management, reporting and audit trails.

18. Razorpay Payment Integration
18.1 Payment Creation
Backend creates the payment order for the authenticated application.
Amount is retrieved from server-side configuration.
Student cannot modify the payable amount from the frontend.
Payment order is tied to Registration Number/Offer ID.
Razorpay checkout is opened for that specific order.
18.2 Payment Verification
Student initiates payment.
Razorpay processes payment.
Frontend may show the immediate result.
Backend verifies the payment using Razorpay server-side mechanisms.
Webhook/event is received and validated.
Transaction is recorded with gateway identifiers.
Payment is marked successful only after verified confirmation.
Offer acceptance/payment state is updated.
Student/admin notifications are triggered.
Portal provisioning workflow starts.
18.3 Security Rules
Never trust a frontend-only success response.
Validate Razorpay webhook signatures.
Use idempotent payment event processing.
Prevent duplicate order/payment application.
Failed/pending/refunded payments must not activate paid access.
Store gateway IDs and status history.
Do not expose secret Razorpay credentials to frontend code.

51. Final Product Vision
AAGAM Digital Technology shall operate a single integrated platform in which public visitors discover internships, training and placement opportunities; students complete a verified application journey; Admin/HR teams review and manage candidates; authorized administrators generate official documents; students accept offers and make applicable payments through a secure Razorpay workflow; verified payment automatically triggers portal provisioning; and the entire internship lifecycle is managed digitally through the student and staff dashboards.
The platform shall prioritize transparency, security, auditability and automation. Official Offer Letters, LORs and Certificates must be generated only through authorized workflows and verified against server-side records. Payment success must be based on gateway verification rather than frontend claims, and student credentials should be delivered through secure activation mechanisms.
